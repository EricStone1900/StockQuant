# W1 Artifact EXPIRED lifecycle convergence

Date: 2026-10-01 (Asia/Shanghai)

The V3.1 ArtifactRef contract now accepts the common lifecycle's `EXPIRED` status and optional `expiresAt`. Research Automation validates timestamps, persists the immutable expiry instant, and upgrades old PostgreSQL tables idempotently. Status transitions are forward-only: same-status replay; `PENDING` to `PUBLISHED` or `FAILED`; `PUBLISHED` to `RETAINED` or `EXPIRED`; and `RETAINED` to `EXPIRED`. PostgreSQL rejects an EXPIRED update before its stored deadline, changes to the reference identity/deadline, and any attempt to revive EXPIRED. The domain helper also requires an injected observation time. The file content adapter refuses to publish/read/size EXPIRED content; Platform's owner/run projection preserves EXPIRED and its download lookup continues to expose only PUBLISHED content. No bytes are removed by this change.
