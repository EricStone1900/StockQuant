# V2.1 C1 online watchlist engineering evidence

Date: 2026-10-17 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Delivered behavior

- Platform derives `ownerId` from the authenticated session. The browser can submit candidates only; it cannot submit portfolio positions or in-flight order facts.
- Platform reads the exact Portfolio account snapshot and Trade in-flight orders, verifies owner/account/namespace/TestRun scope, and requires a PAPER account using FakeBroker before updating Market Data.
- Market Data orders and de-duplicates securities as holdings, in-flight orders, then candidates. Required securities are never truncated; more than 100 blocks the update. Omitted candidates are returned for display.
- Market Data persists the pool by owner/account/TestRun. A namespace conflict is rejected without replacing the prior configuration. The Web acceptance page supports saving and reading the scoped pool and displays count, required count, ordering, omissions, and scope.
