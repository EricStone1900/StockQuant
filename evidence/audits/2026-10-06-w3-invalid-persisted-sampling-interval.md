# W3 persisted scheduler interval fail-closed guard (2026-10-06)

The continuous Paper scheduler restored a persisted `RUNNING` state without validating its sampling interval. An invalid value such as 15 minutes could therefore pass the recovery tick and fail only when sampling timers were scheduled. `start()` now rejects any persisted interval other than the supported 20 or 30 minutes before recovery handlers, persistence writes, or timers can run. A regression test verifies the invalid state produces no handler or store side effects.

The timer callbacks also previously sent their scheduled event directly to the handler even if the process woke well after the target time. Scheduled events now allow at most 60 seconds of wall-clock drift; a more delayed or early callback becomes `RECOVERY_CHECK` with no stale `scheduledFor`, allowing the existing recovery path to record the gap without replaying the missed event. A fake-timer regression advances the injected clock across several market hours and confirms the scheduled event is not delivered.
