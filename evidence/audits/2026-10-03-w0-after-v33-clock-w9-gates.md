# W0 current worktree snapshot

Point-in-time snapshot after the V3.3 full code-suite rerun, clock-injection audit, V3 evidence-gate refresh, and V2 current-snapshot link update. Snapshot JSON and sidecar are excluded from their own inventory.

The snapshot does not imply completion of real RD-Agent/provider execution, W3/O1/O2 observation, Ubuntu deployment/observation, V3.3 business workflow, or human acceptance.
